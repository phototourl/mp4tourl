# Payment follow-up email templates

Support outreach templates for payment / renewal research. Keep tone brief, not salesy. One-line reply is enough.

---

## 1. Incomplete checkout (EditStamp style)

**Use when:** Payment was not completed (failed or abandoned).

**Subject:** EditStamp payment follow-up — your feedback helps us improve

```
Dear Customer,

Thank you for visiting EditStamp. We are reaching out regarding a recent payment that was not completed. This is a brief follow-up to help us improve our product and checkout experience — not a sales message.

Could you let us know which applies to you?

• Payment issue — the transaction failed or could not be completed
• Decided not to proceed — you cancelled because the product did not fully meet your needs yet

A one-line reply is enough. If you experienced a payment problem and would still like to purchase, we are happy to assist.

Thank you for your time.
```

**PhotoToURL variant — subject:** PhotoToURL payment follow-up — your feedback helps us improve  
Replace product name / “visiting” as needed (e.g. “Thank you for being a PhotoToURL customer”).

---

## 2. Renewal with a different card (Visa / auto-renew research)

**Use when:** Customer renewed with a different card; investigating Visa / auto-renew declines.

**Subject:** PhotoToURL payment follow-up — your feedback helps us improve

```
Dear Rich,

Thank you for being a PhotoToURL customer. We're reaching out about a recent subscription renewal — this is a brief follow-up to help us improve our product and payment experience, not a sales message.

We noticed your renewal was completed with a different card. Could you let us know which applies?

• Payment issue — your Visa card was declined during auto-renewal
• Changed card on purpose — you switched cards for another reason

A one-line reply is enough. If you ran into a payment problem, any detail you can share (e.g. bank decline, error message) would really help us fix this for other users.

Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
PhotoToURL Support
support@phototourl.com
```

**Placeholders:** replace `Rich` / greeting with the customer name; adjust brand and support address when reusing for EditStamp.
